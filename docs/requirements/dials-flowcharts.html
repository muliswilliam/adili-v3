Commission & Declarant Onboarding

This flowchart covers how Responsible Commissions and declarants get onto Adili Online. EACC creates each Responsible Commission and assigns its reporting officer. The reporting officer imports the Commission's declarant roster. Declarants then onboard themselves by matching their personnel file number against that roster. No mass invitations are sent to declarants. Onboarding happens once; every later declaration starts from a normal login (Flowchart 2).

Reporting entities: Reporting entities (e.g. schools under TSC, ministries and departments under PSC) are retained on roster records for categorisation, filtering and reporting. They do not access declarations directly and have no role in onboarding. Only the Responsible Commission receives and reviews declarations.

Onboarding rules: A declarant can only onboard against a roster record that their Responsible Commission has imported. One account per roster record. The system never reveals whether a file number exists to someone who fails verification.

Settled points (25 Sep 2026, ADR-014): (1) Match requires personnel file number and national ID. (2) One-time codes go to the contacts on the roster record, shown masked; the declarant enters their own only when the record has none. (3) The IPRS/NRB identity check is kept and runs at profile completion. (4) No fallback for Commissions without a roster; EACC monitors coverage.

Key Legal References: s.32 (Responsible Commissions), s.33 (delegation), s.46 (confidentiality), Reg 5 (Responsible Commissions for other officers), Reg 25(2) (Form M non-filers), Admin Mech. 39 (Commission systems).

Scoped Access — System Design Note: When access is granted, the system must enforce the exact scope approved by the RC. This means the system must support configurable filters on: declaration year(s) requested, inclusion/exclusion of spouse and dependents data, specific sections (income only, assets only, liabilities only, or combinations). Granting access must never expose the full declaration where only a subset was approved. The RC does not part with the original declaration (Admin Mech. 31(2)).
